# Standalone direction selection

Use this only for STANDALONE before compiling the renderer prompt. It chooses a strong bitmap-only art direction without turning Codex's reasoning into a screen template. Candidate work and the selection receipt stay outside ImageGen.

## Build a semantic shadow

Extract only the current output's primary object, supporting evidence, supplied actions/state, exact content and relationships, preserve-only scope, replace-region responsibilities, and user-authorized aesthetic evidence. Reduce a `FUNCTIONAL_REFERENCE` to these facts unless an exact visible invariant requires scoped renderer exposure.

Keep source appearance, rejected visual forms, prior artifacts, and concrete devices mentioned as review examples out of candidate generation. They remain evidence for later review, not ingredients for the next direction.

## Check semantic affordance

Before divergence, verify that the supplied current-output data can support the visual relationship under consideration. A direction that depends on global context, community structure, recurrence, cycles, density contrast, or a highlighted subpath needs truthful topology that actually contains those properties. Do not compensate for a sparse tree, short list, or single chain by inventing background entities, edges, actions, or states.

When a dense exact structure would overwhelm one bitmap or require a renderer prompt dominated by structural data, define an authorized representative design band or choose a direction that fits the bounded content. Keep the complete structure under `mustRemainReachable` and later runtime evidence. Do not silently turn approximate decorative topology into product truth.

## Diverge before selecting

First forecast the small modal neighborhood the renderer is most likely to choose from the semantic shadow alone. Record two or three materially different likely completions when the content admits more than one familiar artifact pattern. Describe each representation signature without copying its concrete visual nouns into later prompts:

- context model — what is treated as persistent context versus current focus;
- relation carrier — which perceptual property primarily communicates relationships;
- focus transition — what changes when attention or selection changes;
- entity embodiment — how participants become visually identifiable without fixing position or component geometry.

Treat every forecast mode as a completion to challenge, not an automatic winner.

Privately compare two directions for a bounded independent bitmap. Compare three when the request is a material redesign, when macro-layout/module-topology/data-visualization form must change, or when the first obvious direction would be highly transferable.

Each candidate states only:

- one product-specific perceptual relationship among the primary object, supporting evidence, and supplied action/state;
- the intended attention choreography and emotional posture;
- coherent typography, color, surface, and imagery roles;
- a representation grammar using the four fields above when structural or relational presentation is material;
- what ImageGen remains free to decide.

Candidates must differ in perceptual organization after removing palette, typography character, materials, effects, and fashionable style words. For structural or relational work, each serious candidate differs from every forecast mode and from every other serious candidate on at least two representation-grammar fields. Record a compact field-by-field distance row naming those changed fields and one observable consequence; a generic claim such as “more editorial” or “less templated” does not prove distance. Do not count restyled versions of the same hierarchy, grouping, sequence, disclosure, or visual metaphor as different directions. Do not specify coordinates, component trees, fixed regions, node placement, exact topology, or connector geometry.

## Adversarial selection gates

Reject a candidate when any gate fails:

- **product relation** — material/style vocabulary or a carrier metaphor does the work instead of a relationship grounded in supplied product meaning;
- **default distance** — the nearest familiar completion for this artifact could satisfy the candidate without changing its perceptual organization;
- **mode distance** — the candidate restates the forecast representation grammar with different art-direction language;
- **artifact identity** — after hiding titles and exact labels, the requested primary product object no longer reads as itself and collapses into another familiar artifact type;
- **source-topology distance** — for a redesign, the candidate preserves the source's non-authoritative role partition, ordering, grouping, or disclosure logic after cosmetic styling is removed;
- **style removal** — removing color, type character, material, effects, and imagery makes the candidate indistinguishable from another candidate or the source;
- **truth** — the candidate needs invented content, behavior, claims, or a broader reference role;
- **semantic affordance** — the supplied topology/content cannot truthfully produce the candidate's perceptual relationship at the requested density;
- **creative openness** — fewer than two materially different compositions or encodings could satisfy it.

Select one winner; do not average candidates. If none passes, revise the directions rather than lengthening the renderer prompt.

## Freeze a private receipt

Before the ImageGen call, record privately:

- selected product-specific relationship;
- forecast modal-neighborhood signatures, nearest defaults, and why none satisfies the winner;
- a field-by-field distance row from the winner to every forecast mode and every surviving candidate;
- for redesigns, the semantic source-topology difference;
- selected context model, relation carrier, focus transition, and entity embodiment when applicable;
- aesthetic role system;
- at least two materially different valid renderer freedoms;
- direction/avoid consistency result.

The receipt is a preflight aid, not visible copy, a durable FULL artifact, or renderer input.

## Compile only the winner

Pass ImageGen the selected relationship, current-output truth, critical labels, applicable render-time inputs, the selected representation grammar, a concise aesthetic role system, explicit creative latitude, and up to three causal failure classes. Do not pass candidates, forecast mode names/categories, rejected forms, the receipt, source-specific negative visual nouns, research rationale, or a ready-made layout. A causal avoid describes the quality failure positively enough to guide review; it does not repeat the modal completion as a negative target.
