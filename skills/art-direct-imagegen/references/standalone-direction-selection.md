# Standalone entry through Product Design

Use this for a standalone frontend UI bitmap before compiling the renderer prompt. Standalone describes the entry request; Product Design still owns direction selection. This reference is a consumer preflight, not a renderer-owned design method.

## Use the actual owner

Read and apply [Frontend Product Design](../../frontend-product-design/SKILL.md), including its required current working-product research. A shared visual-direction or research reference alone is not invocation of the owning skill.

Use its direction-only entry: establish the authorized UI hierarchy, behavior, visual direction, preservation/replacement boundary, output scope, and applicable pre-render evidence, then return to the same Art-Direct caller. Do not recursively dispatch another renderer. A bounded independent bitmap normally uses Product Design STANDARD without code, runtime QA, or durable FULL authority; existing FULL criteria still apply when that scope is actually requested.

If a current scoped Product Design handoff already exists, consume and validate it instead of selecting again. Missing owner skill, inadequate required research, unresolved product decisions, or a contradictory direction is BLOCKED; do not substitute a local direction.

## Validate the returned handoff

Check that it includes:

- the requested primary product object and truthful supporting content, actions, states, and relationships;
- target surface, applicable viewport and output responsibilities, plus the allowed reference roles;
- a Product Design-owned perceptual relationship, attention hierarchy, coherent aesthetic roles, and current comparable-product rationale;
- the representation grammar when relational meaning requires it: context model, relation carrier, focus transition, and entity embodiment;
- a truthful semantic affordance: supplied topology/content actually supports the relationship without invented entities, edges, states, or density;
- preservation/replacement boundaries when redesign is requested, and content distribution when density or scroll requires it;
- a direction reference and lock/SHA when the active profile requires them, or the bounded non-promotable direction receipt;
- a coherent avoid list and enough renderer freedom for more than one feasible composition.

Do not require novelty or a fixed distance quota from familiar implementations. Product Design may select a mature current product pattern when its inspected evidence and task fit justify the choice. Art-Direct checks handoff fidelity rather than selecting a different concept.

A functional reference conveys scoped facts, not automatic style, branding, shell, or layout authority. Do not repair absent semantics with invented product content. An exact dense dataset may require Product Design to declare representative content coverage; the renderer cannot silently reduce or expand it.

## Compile the owned direction

Pass ImageGen only the selected relationship, current-output truth, critical labels, applicable render-time inputs, relevant representation grammar, concise aesthetic roles, explicit creative latitude, and causal failure classes. Keep Product Design's candidate work, research rationale, evidence ledger, and rejected concepts outside the renderer prompt.

Direction-changing feedback returns to Product Design. A bounded artifact defect preserves its direction and follows the existing saved-bitmap edit and attempt/budget rules. The bitmap remains preview-only until a separately authorized downstream handoff establishes implementation scope.
